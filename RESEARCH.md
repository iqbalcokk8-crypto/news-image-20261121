## 6. FBI crime database hacked (United States / cyber)

- Hackers breached the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC)
  database — the nationwide system police use to share warrants,
  background checks and fugitive records. The breach was first
  reported by CNN on Friday 20 Nov; the Associated Press confirmed it
  on Saturday 21 Nov; President Trump commented on Saturday ("not
  good"). The FBI is investigating; the scope was not yet clear. It
  was the second FBI breach in months, after a September theft of
  employee data by ShinyHunters.
- Sources: CNN, 20 Nov 2026 (first report); Associated Press,
  21 Nov 2026 (confirmation); Fox News / USA Today, 21 Nov 2026.

## 7. Dengue breakthrough in Brazil (health / science — the hopeful story)

- Specially bred mosquitoes carrying Wolbachia bacteria cut dengue
  AND chikungunya infections in children by about 69% — the 69% is
  the combined figure for both viruses, measured by blood tests in
  schoolchildren, not a nationwide dengue drop. The EVITA Dengue
  trial ran four years in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, with 5,757 children
  aged 6–11; results were presented at the American Society of
  Tropical Medicine and Hygiene meeting, not yet peer-reviewed.
- Sources: Associated Press, 21 Nov 2026; The Independent (via
  khabr.pk), 21 Nov 2026.

Image wording: "DENGUE & CHIKUNGUNYA CUT 69%" / "Wolbachia
mosquitoes cut dengue and chikungunya infections in children by
about 69%." Source label: "ASSOCIATED PRESS".
