## 1. LEAD — Iran strikes Bahrain (Middle East / conflict)

- Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they struck two US military positions
  in Bahrain, at al-Riffa and Awali.
- Bahrain said the attack hit a civilian industrial facility, starting a
  fire but injuring no one, and that its air defences intercepted other
  attacks.
- Iran also said it downed a US drone near Qeshm Island; the US denied
  Iran's claim that it hit an F-16.
- Sources: Reuters (via Majik Morning Brief, 21 Nov); One Turn day page
  21 Nov 2026; Candid Dragon evening briefing 21 Nov.

## 2. Mexico captures "El Chapo Isidro" (Latin America / crime)

- Mexican special forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as
  "Chapo Isidro", in Guasave, Sinaloa. He was on the FBI's ten
  most-wanted list.
- At least 11 people died — four soldiers and seven attackers — and the
  arrest set off gunfights, fires and road blockades; baseball fans fled
  gunfire and the weekend's remaining games were cancelled. The US warned
  travellers in Sinaloa.
- Sources: Reuters; Associated Press via NPR; CNN via KVIA, 21 Nov 2026.

Image wording: "MEXICO CAPTURES 'EL CHAPO ISIDRO'" / "FBI
most-wanted cartel boss seized in Sinaloa; at least 11 dead as

## 3. Russian missile hits Kryvyi Rih (Europe / war)

- A Russian ballistic missile hit the centre of Kryvyi Rih, President
  Zelenskyy's hometown, on Saturday, killing a boy aged 7 and a
  paramedic aged 20, and wounding dozens (28 remained in hospital;
  later counts up to 58 wounded). A second strike hit as rescuers
  arrived — a triple-tap attack; 23 apartment buildings and a hospital
  were damaged.
- Russian missiles also came down on Polish territory during the same
  barrage; NATO condemned the violation.
- Sources: Reuters; CNN via LA Post; Institute for the Study of War,
  Russian Offensive Campaign Assessment, 21 Nov 2026; SENTINEL brief.

Image wording: "MISSILE HITS ZELENSKYY'S HOMETOWN" / "A Russian

wounded. Missiles also landed in Poland."
